It operates natively on QGIS 4 without external Remotior Sensus dependencies, retains classic SCP Band Set, ROI/Signature, Classification, Band Calc, and Postprocessing workflows, and adapts Sentinel search and download to the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem. Validated on QGIS 4.2.3 (Qt 6 / Python 3.14).
